# 产品分类标准

## 首期主类

- Industrial Control Components
- PLC and PAC Systems
- HMI and Industrial Visualization
- Variable Frequency Drives and Motion Control
- Contactors, Relays and Protection
- Switchgear and Power Distribution Components
- Industrial Sensors and Instrumentation
- Measurement and Analytics Instruments
- Industrial Communication and Networking
- Control Panels and Electrical Assemblies

## 每个产品实体的标准字段

品牌、产品类别、产品族、型号/料号、简短用途、关键规格、应用、可替代型号、数据表、图片、原厂页面、授权状态、区域供应状态、库存/交期、更新时间。

除品牌、类别和业务概述外，其他字段先保留为待补数据；没有数据时不显示空的技术承诺。
